v0.2.10 Reporting Artifact Contract

Adds a shared reporting contract for Meetup and Scene runs: date-scoped session logs are the source of truth, while minutes, draft output, and round reports are optional exports controlled by the user's runtime profile.

version v0.2.10 session log first TXT/HTML exports Hermes-customizable no forced delivery

Patch Intent

Users and Hermes runtimes can choose which reports to auto-generate, but WSA now exposes a consistent contract so outputs are labeled and reconstructable. The patch does not make WSA deliver chat messages or store every report format by default.

1. Session LogDate-scoped run log is the source of truth.
2. MinutesHuman-readable meeting or scene-session record.
3. Draft OutputMeetup conclusion or scene manuscript draft.
4. Round ReportScheduler, quality gate, rejection, rollback, and stop reason trace.
5. On DemandRuntime/profile decides whether and when to export TXT/HTML.

Code Added

  • reporting_contract.py with wsa.reporting.artifact_contract.v1.
  • Runtime registry exposes reporting_artifact_policy.
  • Orchestrator session contracts and bridge next payloads include reporting guidance.
  • Meeting transcripts include the same reporting contract.

Artifacts

  • human_session_minutes: meeting/scene real-session log for humans.
  • draft_output: manuscript draft or meetup conclusion.
  • round_orchestration_report: round/checkpoint operational trace.
  • All recommended exports support TXT and HTML.

Boundaries

  • WSA does not force automatic export.
  • WSA does not own chat delivery preferences.
  • Session logs may contain private world data and must not be committed to the public template repo.
  • Deleting or archiving session logs remains a runtime/operator policy decision.

Artifact Isolation

  • Derived outputs should stay under managed workspace roots such as worlds/<world_id>/artifacts/, reports, scenes, meetings, or Hermes outbox paths.
  • Out-of-contract files require an artifact_source_map.json manifest.
  • The source map records origin run/session, artifact type, path, manager, cleanup hint, and whether it is safe to delete with the session.
  • The goal is easy install, migration, archive, and uninstall without manual artifact hunting.
